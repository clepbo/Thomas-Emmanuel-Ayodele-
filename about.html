Hi, I'm Thomas Emmanuel Ayodele — TEA. My six years in the design industry have allowed me to inhabit many roles: a brand designer working in-house at startups, a contractor at branding agencies, and a freelancer working one-on-one with founders who care deeply about the marks they leave on the world.

Working across these different fields has enabled me to pick up several skills alongside graphic and brand design, such as motion design, illustration, 3D, copywriting, type design and creative direction. However, I have honed my expertise most sharply in branding and brand identity design.

I combine my experience in graphic design and these disciplines to solve problems, tell stories, and help brands discover their truest selves. Through consistent practice, I've developed a systematic and meticulous approach that has yielded exceptional results for clients and their businesses.
